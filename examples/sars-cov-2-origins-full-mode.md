# SARS-CoV-2 origins: a Convergence Guard Full Mode case study

[Русская версия](sars-cov-2-origins-full-mode.ru.md)

> **Methodological case-study disclaimer.** This document is a worked example of Convergence Guard, run on 2026-10-06 against publicly available evidence. It is **not** original laboratory research, a field epidemiology investigation, a forensic examination, a criminal investigation, an intelligence assessment, or a finding of legal responsibility. The run had no privileged access to laboratory records, classified intelligence, private health records, unpublished biological samples, or other non-public evidence. Its conclusions are therefore claims about what the cited public evidence supports under the protocol's rules, not a substitute for primary scientific or investigative work.

## Question

Which causal scenario for the origin of SARS-CoV-2 and the start of the COVID-19 pandemic is best supported by the totality of publicly available evidence as of 6 October 2026? Are the data sufficient to prefer one causal scenario with defensible confidence, or is the correct result `INSUFFICIENT DATA TO CHOOSE`?

The run did **not** begin from an exhaustive list of named public hypotheses. It was required to generate causally distinct model families from the evidence and preserve an outside alternative if the public record allowed a materially different pathway.

## Why this case is useful for Convergence Guard

The question combines several failure modes the protocol is designed to resist:

- politically and institutionally contested sources;
- incomplete and sometimes inaccessible primary records;
- several analyses that reuse the same underlying samples or case lists;
- strong temptation to confuse capability with occurrence;
- strong temptation to confuse an early amplification site with the site of first infection;
- genomic observations that are compatible with more than one causal pathway;
- intelligence assessments whose underlying evidence is partly classified;
- missing data that can be misused either as proof of absence or as proof of concealment.

The central methodological problem is therefore not merely “which side has more arguments?” It is whether any surviving model closes the decisive causal transitions with evidence that is sufficiently direct, discriminating, inspectable, and independent.

## No solo baseline

Unlike the first public case study in this repository, this run deliberately used **no preliminary solo-analysis baseline**. The user explicitly required a new independent Full Mode run from scratch and prohibited prior conclusions from being passed into isolation-dependent stages.

That matters because a solo baseline, even if later hidden, can influence mandate design or coordinator expectations. Here the search began from a shared factual brief and a decision contract, not from a preselected favorite.

# Phase A — evidence brief and decision contract

## Decision contract

**Decision question:** Which causal family is best supported by publicly inspectable evidence, and is that support sufficient for defensible causal attribution?

**Scope:** Separate at least three stages that are often collapsed into one story:

1. **proximal viral ancestry / acquisition context** — where the virus or its immediate precursor came from biologically;
2. **first causally relevant human infection** — how the lineage that established the pandemic entered a human;
3. **sustained transmission / amplification** — how that infection became a self-sustaining outbreak and where early amplification occurred.

A model may therefore combine natural viral ancestry with a research-associated exposure, or a first infection outside the Huanan market with later market amplification.

**Forbidden substitutions:**

- research capability → occurrence of a research accident;
- market amplification → proof of first spillover at the market;
- source reputation → truth of the underlying claim;
- classified or unavailable support → confirmed fact;
- absence of an identified intermediate host → proof of a laboratory origin;
- absence of a public laboratory incident record → proof that no laboratory incident occurred;
- repeated publication of one dataset → independent corroboration.

**Success criterion:** A preferred model should explain observations that nearby alternatives do not explain equally well, survive provenance and common-ancestry correction, and close its load-bearing causal transitions better than competitors.

**Cost of error:** A false attribution can distort scientific policy, biosafety policy, wildlife-trade policy, diplomacy, public trust, and responsibility claims. Preserving uncertainty is preferable to converting plausibility into fact.

## Claim-level source policy

The run evaluated claims rather than treating whole institutions, journals, governments, activist groups, or intelligence bodies as globally reliable or unreliable.

For material claims it asked, where practical:

- What is the nearest available primary source?
- Can the underlying evidence be inspected?
- Are methods and raw data available?
- How close in time is the observation to the event?
- Do several publications descend from the same dataset or briefing?
- What contradictory evidence exists?
- What incentives or conflicts could affect reporting or selection?
- Is the source functioning here as `EVIDENCE`, `CORROBORATION`, `CONTEXT`, `LEAD ONLY`, or `UNSUPPORTED`?

A recurring distinction was:

> **CONFIRMED:** an institution or document states X  
> is not the same as  
> **CONFIRMED:** X itself occurred.

## Main factual anchors

### 1. Huanan was an exceptionally early outbreak node

Worobey et al. reported that the earliest known December 2019 COVID-19 cases, including cases without a reported direct market link, were geographically centered around the Huanan Seafood Wholesale Market, where live SARS-CoV-2-susceptible mammals were sold in late 2019.

This is meaningful evidence that Huanan was a very early epidemiological and amplification node. It does **not by itself identify the first animal-to-human transmission event**.

Source: [Worobey et al., *Science* (2022)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9348750/).

### 2. Market environmental samples were positive; sampled animals were not

China CDC reported 923 environmental samples collected after market closure, with SARS-CoV-2 detected in 74, and 457 animal-related samples from 18 species, all negative for SARS-CoV-2 nucleic acid.

The negative animal results are genuine negative evidence. Their discriminating power is limited because sampling began after closure and did not constitute a complete pre-closure sample of the live susceptible animals sold during the causally relevant period.

Source: [Liu et al., *Nature* (2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06043-2).

### 3. Susceptible-wildlife genetic material co-occurred in some positive market samples

A 2024 *Cell* analysis of the China CDC market metagenomic data identified genetic material from wildlife including raccoon dogs, civets, and bamboo rats in SARS-CoV-2-positive samples from a wildlife stall, and found elevated SARS-CoV-2 positivity near that stall.

This strengthens the evidence that susceptible wildlife and virus-contaminated environments occupied the same early market micro-environment. Because the samples were environmental and collected after substantial human transmission, it does **not establish that a sampled animal was infected or establish direction of transmission**.

Source: [Crits-Christoph et al., *Cell* (2024)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11427129/).

### 4. The strongest market datasets have shared ancestry

Much of the Huanan environmental, metagenomic, wildlife-DNA, and stall-localization evidence descends from the same post-closure China CDC specimen corpus. Reanalysis can add information, correct methods, and reveal previously unused features, but it does not turn one biological sampling event into several independent sampling events.

Likewise, several early-case spatial analyses reuse overlapping early Wuhan case records.

### 5. Spatial localization is contested

Published statistical critiques argue that the Huanan-centered case pattern does not by itself identify the market as the origin and may be sensitive to case ascertainment and spatial-method assumptions.

Sources: [Stoyan & Chiu, JRSS A (2024)](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssa/article/187/3/710/7557954); [Weissman, JRSS A (2024)](https://academic.oup.com/jrsssa/article/187/3/720/7632556).

These critiques weaken a strong claim of **first spillover at Huanan** more than they weaken the narrower claim that Huanan was a major early outbreak node.

### 6. Early lineage A/B evidence is informative but not load-bearing

Pekar et al. argued that early SARS-CoV-2 diversity was most consistent with at least two cross-species transmissions. A 2023 erratum corrected a code error that had overstated Bayes factors for multiple introductions and emphasized disagreement in rooting approaches, while the corrected analysis still favored multiple introductions.

Sources: [Pekar et al., *Science* (2022)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9348752/); [2023 erratum](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13244787/).

The run therefore treated “two early lineages” as potentially supporting repeated spillover, but not as a decisive origin identifier.

### 7. Natural bat-sarbecovirus ancestry is strongly supported but poorly discriminating between some routes

Field studies have documented extensive natural diversity and recombination among bat SARS-related coronaviruses, including viruses capable of using human ACE2 or with related receptor-binding properties.

This strongly supports natural evolutionary ancestry. But natural ancestry is compatible with both ordinary wildlife exposure and research-associated exposure to a naturally occurring virus.

Sources include [Hu et al., *PLOS Pathogens* (2017)](https://journals.plos.org/plospathogens/article?id=10.1371/journal.ppat.1006698) and [Temmam et al., *Nature* (2022)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-022-04532-4).

### 8. Relevant coronavirus field and laboratory work existed before the outbreak

Public pre-pandemic publications establish that Wuhan Institute of Virology researchers and collaborators sampled bat coronaviruses, isolated live SARS-related viruses, used cell culture, and used reverse-genetics systems in related coronavirus research.

This is direct evidence for **capability and exposure pathways**. It is not direct evidence that SARS-CoV-2 or a sufficiently close progenitor was present in a laboratory before the outbreak or that a worker was infected.

Sources: [Hu et al. (2017)](https://journals.plos.org/plospathogens/article?id=10.1371/journal.ppat.1006698); [Menachery et al., PNAS (2016)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26976607/); [WIV1 reverse-genetics work, *Journal of Virology* (2016)](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jvi.03079-15).

### 9. Laboratory adaptation is biologically possible; occurrence in the SARS-CoV-2 chain is not established

A 2025 *PLOS Pathogens* paper reported that the bat sarbecovirus WIV1-CoV likely acquired an adaptive spike mutation during propagation in cell culture. This demonstrates that laboratory propagation can produce consequential adaptation in a relevant virus class.

It is `CORROBORATION` for mechanism feasibility, not evidence that SARS-CoV-2 followed that path.

Source: [Tse et al., *PLOS Pathogens* (2025)](https://journals.plos.org/plospathogens/article?id=10.1371/journal.ppat.1013123).

### 10. DEFUSE establishes a proposal, not execution

The 2018 DEFUSE proposal is material because it documents proposed experimental work on bat coronaviruses, including cleavage-site-related work. But a proposal is evidence of research intent/capability, not evidence that the proposed work was performed or that it produced SARS-CoV-2. The publicly released DARPA program-manager summary did not recommend the proposal for funding.

Public document copies: [DEFUSE proposal](https://usrtk.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/defuse-proposal.pdf); [DARPA PM summary](https://drasticresearch.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/hr00118s017-preempt-fp-019-pm-summary-selectable-not-recommended.pdf).

### 11. Public intelligence assessments establish assessments, not their hidden evidence

The 2023 ODNI report documented divided U.S. intelligence-community views, WIV coronavirus research and biosafety concerns, while stating that the agencies did not have direct evidence of a specific research-related incident and did not know of a pre-pandemic WIV holding of SARS-CoV-2 or a close progenitor.

Source: [ODNI, *Potential Links Between the Wuhan Institute of Virology and the Origins of COVID-19* (2023)](https://archive.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/Report-on-Potential-Links-Between-the-Wuhan-Institute-of-Virology-and-the-Origins-of-COVID-19-20230623.pdf).

In June 2026, ODNI released additional materials under a press release that asserted a Wuhan-research origin and alleged manipulation of earlier intelligence processes. The existence and wording of that assessment are confirmed; where underlying evidence remains non-public, the substantive event itself cannot be upgraded to `CONFIRMED` merely from the institutional assertion.

Source: [ODNI release, 18 June 2026](https://archive.dni.gov/index.php/newsroom/press-releases/press-releases-2026/4166-pr-11-26).

### 12. Important data needed to test all major routes remain unavailable

The 2025 WHO SAGO assessment stated that the weight of evidence it reviewed suggested zoonotic spillover, while also stating that information needed to assess a laboratory-related event had not been made available and that such an event could therefore neither be confirmed nor eliminated. SAGO also identified missing early human sequences, animal/supply-chain information, laboratory-work documentation, biosafety records, and staff-health information.

Source: [WHO SAGO, *Independent assessment of the origins of SARS-CoV-2* (2025)](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/independent-assessment-of-the-origins-of-sars-cov-2-from-the-scientific-advisory-group-for-the-origins-of-novel-pathogens).

The report was treated as a transparent synthesis and a source for what SAGO reviewed and requested, not as an authority whose conclusion automatically determined the result.

# Phase A reframe — what exactly is being explained?

A fresh reframe reviewer identified a hidden assumption in the ordinary public framing: “viral origin” and “the start of sustained human transmission” are often treated as one indivisible event.

The stronger formulation was to infer separately:

1. proximal viral ancestry or acquisition context;
2. first causally relevant human infection;
3. establishment and amplification of sustained human transmission.

This reframe matters because a natural virus can enter humans through research contact, and a first infection away from Huanan can still be followed by major amplification at Huanan.

The reviewer also required preservation of an outside/hybrid route because public evidence does not guarantee that any familiar named scenario is exhaustive.

# Phase B — isolated causal search

Three fresh search mandates were designed around different causal identification regions rather than advocacy positions.

## Mandate 1 — entry ecology and supply networks

Test where and how a naturally circulating virus could enter humans through wildlife trade, farms, traders, transport, vendors, markets, or a distributed supply network. Distinguish first infection from later market amplification.

## Mandate 2 — research-contact pathways

Test field-sampling exposure, transport/handling of samples, laboratory exposure to an unmodified natural virus, culture/passaging/adaptation, and targeted recombinant work. Separate evidence of capability from evidence that an event occurred.

## Mandate 3 — genomic and early-transmission identification

Ask which origin transitions can actually be identified from early genomes, phylogeny, molecular features, root uncertainty, early lineages, and timing — and which observations are equally expected under competing acquisition routes.

A research-contact branch was repeated in a fresh context during run recovery. Duplicate worker agreement was **not** counted as independent evidence.

## Coverage gate

The initial search covered three materially different regions:

- wildlife/trade entry and supply ecology;
- research-associated exposure mechanisms;
- genomic/early-transmission identification.

The gate found no missing causal region that justified adding workers merely to increase count. The open-world outside alternative was preserved separately.

# Phase C — neutral candidates, screening, and blind causal mapping

Eight neutral candidate IDs were created before reduction:

| ID | Candidate mechanism |
|---|---|
| N1 | Direct wildlife spillover at or near Huanan → market amplification |
| N2 | Upstream wildlife/supply-chain spillover → infected human or animal reaches Huanan → amplification |
| N3 | Distributed wildlife/supply-network spillovers → Huanan becomes the dominant amplification/detection node |
| N4 | Researcher infected during field sampling by a naturally occurring virus → Wuhan transmission |
| N5 | Laboratory exposure to an unmodified naturally occurring virus → Wuhan transmission |
| N6 | Laboratory exposure after culture/passaging/animal adaptation → Wuhan transmission |
| N7 | Laboratory exposure involving targeted recombinant/chimeric/engineered work → Wuhan transmission |
| N8 | First infection occurs elsewhere by an unresolved mechanism → human introduction to Huanan |

A fresh contract screener and fresh blind causal mapper ran independently. Neither saw the other's output before freezing.

## Screening result

The screener found:

- **N1:** mixed support, partial causal chain, identification threatened by directionality and ascertainment;
- **N2:** mixed support, but the upstream source and first infection remain unidentified;
- **N3:** weaker support because repeated spillovers add an additional evidentiary burden and the A/B inference is corrected/root-sensitive;
- **N4/N5:** mechanistically coherent but weakly supported as events because the specific virus→exposure→infection chain is absent publicly;
- **N6/N7:** weak event-level support and additional unsupported bridges for adaptation/manipulation plus exposure;
- **N8:** failed as a complete causal explanation because it leaves the acquisition mechanism unspecified, but remained useful as an outside residual.

A recurring danger flag for research-associated models was **capability → occurrence**. A recurring danger flag for the direct-Huanan model was **market association → first spillover**.

## Blind causal map

The mapper preserved the internal distinctions and organized the viable models into three higher-order families:

### F-WILDLIFE

`N1 + N2 + N3`, treated as an explicit family of alternative wildlife/trade routes rather than pooled as if all were simultaneously proven.

### F-RESEARCH-NATURAL

`N4 + N5`: research-associated exposure to a naturally occurring virus, either in the field or after an unmodified virus/sample reaches a laboratory.

### F-RESEARCH-ALTERED

`N6 + N7`: research-associated exposure where culture/passaging/adaptation or targeted genetic manipulation is a causal step.

### N8-R — outside / hybrid / unknown

Preserved as the open-world complement. It was not allowed to gain support merely because named models were incomplete.

## Boundary audit

A fresh boundary critic found that the three higher-order families could serve as the finalist slate only if their internal submodels remained visible and support was not pooled across mutually exclusive subroutes.

It also fixed the endpoint: the main question is the route that produced the **pandemic-establishing lineage**, while the literal first-ever SARS-CoV-2 infection in any human remains a separate sensitivity question.

# Phase D — independent finalist dossiers

## Finalist A — wildlife/trade-associated natural entry

### Mechanism

A naturally evolved bat sarbecovirus lineage reaches humans through wildlife ecology and trade, directly at Huanan or upstream in the supply chain; Huanan then becomes a major early amplification node.

### Strongest support

The strongest bundle is event-proximate:

1. early known cases concentrated around Huanan;
2. susceptible live mammals were sold there shortly before closure;
3. SARS-CoV-2 was widespread in post-closure market environmental samples;
4. susceptible-wildlife DNA occurred in some SARS-CoV-2-positive wildlife-stall samples;
5. natural bat-sarbecovirus ecology supplies a biologically coherent upstream reservoir.

This bundle does more than establish generic possibility. It places susceptible wildlife, virus contamination, and an early human outbreak node in the same causal neighborhood.

### Strongest contrary evidence / limitations

- no pre-closure market animal has been publicly shown to be infected with SARS-CoV-2;
- no direct progenitor or intermediate-host virus has been identified;
- all 457 sampled animal-related specimens were negative;
- environmental samples were collected after substantial human transmission, so human shedding can reproduce much of the signal;
- early-case spatial inference is vulnerable to incomplete ascertainment and method criticism;
- upstream farms, traders, transporters, and vendors have not been publicly traced into a complete infection chain.

### What it explains better than the nearest competitors

It explains the conjunction of early Huanan epidemiology, actual susceptible wildlife sales, virus-positive wildlife-stall environments, and wildlife genetic material **without first requiring an undocumented research possession/exposure event**.

### What it does not uniquely explain

A natural-looking genome, Huanan amplification, post-closure market contamination, failure to find the source years later, and rapid human-to-human spread are all compatible with some research-associated routes as well.

### What would materially strengthen it

- an authenticated pre-closure infected animal specimen;
- a basal SARS-CoV-2 or near-progenitor sequence from a traced animal or supply-chain node;
- supplier/vendor/transporter infection predating known market transmission with a genomic and provenance link.

### What would materially weaken it

- a robust, well-documented sustained human chain clearly predating Huanan and unrelated to wildlife trade;
- comprehensive archived animal/supplier data covering the relevant period that are genuinely negative;
- a directly evidenced alternative transition from precursor to first pandemic-establishing human infection.

## Finalist B — research-associated exposure to a naturally occurring virus

### Mechanism

A naturally occurring precursor is encountered during field sampling or reaches a research facility; a researcher or associated worker becomes infected without a causal engineering/adaptation step and seeds ordinary human transmission. Huanan may still become an early amplification node.

### Strongest support

Public evidence directly establishes:

- extensive bat-coronavirus field sampling;
- sample handling and transport;
- isolation and culture of live SARS-related coronaviruses;
- a real occupational exposure pathway;
- biosafety concerns and laboratory work under containment levels that are relevant to risk assessment.

This family also provides a mechanism by which researchers based in Wuhan could encounter viruses from distant bat ecologies.

### Strongest contrary evidence / limitations

The decisive event bridge is missing publicly:

`relevant natural precursor → documented research possession/exposure → infected worker → onward transmission`.

No publicly verified pre-outbreak SARS-CoV-2/near-progenitor holding, staff seroconversion with matching sequence, specific incident record, or traced worker-to-community chain closes that transition.

### What it explains better than wildlife/trade routes

It explains how a rare bat virus from distant field sites could reach a Wuhan-associated human without requiring a documented local wildlife animal to carry it into the city.

### What both families explain equally well

Natural viral ancestry, a genome without a required engineering signature, Huanan amplification after introduction, and failure to recover a positive market animal after the relevant animals were removed.

### What would materially strengthen it

An authenticated, dated pre-December-2019 field or laboratory sample with a near-progenitor sequence plus matched staff exposure, seroconversion, clinical specimen, or incident/access record.

### What would materially weaken it

Complete and independently auditable holdings/raw-read/field-roster/staff-serology records that exclude a relevant exposure in the critical window, especially together with a direct animal-source chain.

## Finalist C — research-associated altered/passaged/engineered virus

### Mechanism

A natural precursor is acquired through research and is then altered through culture/passaging/animal adaptation or targeted recombinant work; an exposure event infects a human and seeds transmission.

### Strongest support

The public record establishes:

- relevant reverse-genetics and live-virus capability;
- proposed experimental designs such as DEFUSE;
- the biological possibility of cell-culture adaptation in bat sarbecoviruses;
- a general accident-risk pathway.

### Strongest contrary evidence / limitations

This family must close every bridge required by the natural-virus research family **plus an additional alteration step**. Public evidence does not currently provide the chain:

`near progenitor → specific alteration/passaging history → SARS-CoV-2-like intermediate → exposure → infected worker → pandemic seeding`.

DEFUSE is a proposal, not proof of execution. The furin cleavage site and other genomic features are not uniquely diagnostic of engineering. Conversely, the lack of an obvious engineering signature does not exclude all laboratory routes, especially the natural-virus family.

### What would materially strengthen it

Authenticated pre-outbreak construct, passage, raw-read, freezer, or animal-work records that genetically bridge a known precursor to SARS-CoV-2, together with evidence of exposure and onward transmission.

### What would materially weaken it

A complete audited research inventory and raw-data record excluding the relevant precursor and experimental lineage, or a directly documented wildlife-source chain that predates human transmission.

# Shared-bias and source-dependency audit

The slate-level audit found that apparent evidence volume on both sides is inflated by shared ancestry.

## Market branch

```text
China CDC post-closure Huanan samples
→ Nature 2023 surveillance paper
→ metagenomic / wildlife-DNA reanalyses
→ stall-localization and host-provenance analyses
```

These are not independent biological collections.

## Early-case branch

```text
overlapping early Wuhan case records
→ geospatial analyses
→ reanalyses and statistical critiques
```

Different statistical papers can disagree usefully, but they do not create new patients.

## Early-genome branch

```text
sparse overlapping early human sequences
→ lineage/root inference
→ multiple-introduction models
→ corrections and reanalyses
```

Rooting and sampling assumptions propagate through the branch.

## Research-origin branch

```text
field/lab programs + publications + grants/proposals
→ media / congressional / institutional summaries
→ intelligence and policy assessments
```

Many downstream claims independently confirm that relevant programs existed, but repetition does not independently establish the pandemic-producing incident.

## Institutional assessments

Different government agencies can also share intelligence streams. Unless common ancestry is known, the run did not assume either full independence or total dependence. Where the underlying evidence is classified, the assessment itself is confirmed but its hidden support remains inaccessible.

# Assumption sensitivity

The decisive sensitivity was not the exact number of early spillovers. The wildlife family remained viable even if the two-introduction argument was discounted.

The most load-bearing assumption was whether the Huanan evidence contains **some information about the upstream animal/trade entry process**, rather than being entirely the product of downstream human amplification plus ascertainment.

- If Huanan wildlife/environment evidence contains genuine upstream signal, the wildlife/trade family retains a material evidential advantage.
- If the Huanan pattern is entirely explainable by downstream amplification, human shedding, and ascertainment, the relative advantage collapses substantially and the result moves closer to complete non-identification.

For research-associated families, the most load-bearing assumption is not whether relevant work was possible — that is established — but whether an event-specific precursor→exposure→infection bridge existed. Public data do not currently resolve that assumption.

# Pairwise collisions

## Wildlife vs research-natural

**Different prediction:** wildlife predicts a traceable infected animal/supply-chain bridge; research-natural predicts an authenticated precursor in field/lab research plus an occupational infection bridge.

**Observations explained by both:** natural ancestry, a non-engineered genome, Huanan amplification, post-closure market contamination.

**Current model preference:** wildlife/trade, because its positive evidence is closer in space and time to the recognized early outbreak and does not rely only on capability.

**Flip observation:** a near-progenitor in a pre-outbreak research sample plus credible exposure/infection evidence would strongly favor research-natural; an infected ancestral animal with traceable supply provenance would strongly favor wildlife.

## Wildlife vs research-altered

**Different prediction:** wildlife predicts an animal/supply-chain bridge; research-altered predicts precursor holdings plus construct/passage/intermediate records and exposure.

**Observation with low discriminating value:** the furin cleavage site alone. It is not a unique marker of either route.

**Current model preference:** wildlife/trade.

## Research-natural vs research-altered

**Different prediction:** altered-virus models require evidence of a causal alteration/adaptation history; natural-virus research exposure does not.

**Current result:** `UNDETERMINED` between these two conditional on a research-associated event, because the public record does not establish the event-specific precursor in either case and does not establish a SARS-CoV-2-producing alteration chain.

# Second-opinion trigger

A fresh independent second opinion was triggered because:

- the apparent leading family depends materially on a contested and internally correlated Huanan evidence complex;
- decisive animal, early-human, laboratory, and staff data remain missing;
- early phylogenetic evidence is sensitive to rooting and sampling;
- institutional assessments conflict while parts of their evidentiary bases are inaccessible;
- the consequences of over-attribution are high.

The second-opinion reviewer reconstructed the inference from raw provenance and neutral candidate claims rather than receiving a preferred winner.

Its result preserved the same key distinction:

> **Wildlife/trade-associated natural entry is the best-supported family in the public record, but the evidence does not close the causal chain strongly enough for conclusive family-level attribution.**

# Phase E — model judgment and evidence-sufficiency judgment

Convergence Guard requires these to be stated separately.

## Model judgment

**Best-supported family:** wildlife/trade-associated natural entry, with Huanan as a major very-early amplification node.

Within that family, the public evidence does **not** reliably distinguish:

- first spillover at Huanan;
- spillover upstream in the wildlife supply chain followed by introduction to Huanan;
- a more distributed multi-spillover supply-network process.

Research-associated exposure to a naturally occurring virus remains a live alternative because relevant field and laboratory exposure pathways existed and critical records needed to test the event are not publicly available.

Research-associated altered/passaged/engineered routes are also mechanistically possible but carry an additional unobserved causal step and currently have weaker event-specific public support.

The outside/hybrid/unknown alternative remains open and receives no support merely by subtraction.

## Evidence-sufficiency judgment

**`INSUFFICIENT DATA TO CHOOSE` for defensible causal attribution of the pandemic-establishing route.**

This is not a claim that all families are equally supported.

The result is:

1. the wildlife/trade family has the strongest **relative** public evidence;
2. its decisive animal→human bridge is still unobserved;
3. key evidence needed to test research-associated routes remains inaccessible or absent publicly;
4. therefore relative preference does not cross the protocol's threshold for a confident causal attribution.

No numerical probabilities were assigned because the public evidence does not support defensible precision.

# Popular arguments weakened by provenance or discriminability checks

### “Huanan was statistically the origin, therefore the first spillover happened there.”

Too strong. Huanan is strongly supported as an early outbreak node; spatial clustering alone does not prove the first infection site, and published critiques identify ascertainment and spatial-identification problems.

### “Raccoon-dog DNA in a positive swab proves an infected raccoon dog.”

Too strong. It proves co-location of wildlife genetic material and SARS-CoV-2 in an environmental sample. It does not establish which organism shed the viral RNA or the direction of transmission.

### “All 457 animal samples were negative, therefore there was no animal source.”

Too strong. The negative samples matter, but they were collected after closure and do not form a complete sample of the live animals present in the critical pre-closure window or of the upstream supply chain.

### “Lineages A and B prove two zoonotic spillovers.”

Weaker than the original 2022 presentation suggested. The corrected analysis still favors multiple introductions, but a code correction reduced Bayes-factor strength and rooting disagreement remains relevant.

### “DEFUSE proves SARS-CoV-2 was engineered.”

No. DEFUSE proves that particular research ideas were proposed. It does not prove execution, creation of SARS-CoV-2, exposure, or pandemic seeding.

### “The furin cleavage site proves engineering.”

No. It can be discussed under both natural-evolution and laboratory scenarios, but it is not uniquely diagnostic on the public evidence.

### “No obvious engineering signature proves there was no laboratory route.”

No. Research-associated exposure to a naturally occurring virus requires no engineering at all, and some reverse-genetics approaches need not leave a simple diagnostic signature.

### “An intelligence agency prefers X, so X is confirmed.”

No. What is confirmed is that the agency made the assessment with a stated confidence level. Classified or otherwise unavailable support cannot be treated as independently inspected evidence in this public run.

# Highest-value decision-changing evidence

The single most valuable practical evidence class is:

> **an authenticated pre-outbreak biological specimen with verifiable provenance, raw sequence data, and exposure metadata that bridges a precursor to the first pandemic-establishing human infection.**

For a wildlife route, this could be a pre-closure animal or upstream supply-chain specimen carrying a basal SARS-CoV-2/near-progenitor lineage with a traceable path to early human infection.

For a research-associated route, it could be a dated pre-outbreak field/laboratory sample or construct with a near-progenitor sequence plus matched worker exposure, seroconversion/clinical sequence, incident/access records, and an onward transmission link.

This evidence class has much higher decision value than additional commentary, additional institutional confidence statements, or more reanalysis of the same post-closure market samples.

# Runtime integrity note

This run used fresh isolated worker contexts for the stages whose value depended on blindness: causal search, screening, mapping, boundary review, finalist dossiers, slate adjudication, and second opinion.

The Chat On Steroids runtime profile requires an isolation smoke test. One initial parent-boundary smoke-test worker timed out before reporting. The boundary was subsequently rechecked, sibling isolation was checked, and the persistence positive control confirmed that revived workers retain their own history and therefore must not be reused for blind roles. The timed-out check is disclosed here rather than silently omitted.

During run recovery, some analytical roles were repeated in fresh contexts. Agreement between repeated workers was never counted as independent real-world evidence; only evidence provenance and the frozen reasoning artifacts were used.

# What this example demonstrates about the method

The most important methodological result is not a new factual claim about SARS-CoV-2. It is the separation of three statements that are often collapsed:

> **Natural bat-sarbecovirus ancestry is strongly supported.**  
> is not the same proposition as  
> **the pandemic-establishing infection occurred through ordinary wildlife trade.**  
> and neither is the same proposition as  
> **the exact first spillover occurred at Huanan.**

Likewise:

> **relevant coronavirus research and exposure pathways existed**  
> is not the same proposition as  
> **a research-associated infection caused the pandemic.**

Once those hidden merges are removed, the public record supports a **relative preference** for the wildlife/trade family while still failing the stronger evidence-sufficiency threshold for causal attribution.
