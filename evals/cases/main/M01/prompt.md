# M01 — Packaging-line label rejection

A parcel packaging line began routing nearly every carton to manual inspection shortly after a label-template update. The same scanners and conveyor hardware had been operating normally earlier in the shift.

Determine what causal conclusion is justified by the supplied evidence and what action should be taken now.

Constraints:

- restore normal throughput quickly;
- do not replace functioning hardware without evidence;
- template rollback is reversible;
- do not use the open web;
- do not assume facts outside the supplied evidence.

Return the required normalized final-answer object.
