# M06 — Temperature excursions across cold-storage zones

A distribution center reports a single site-level 'temperature excursion' incident. Two clusters of zones are warm, and operations wants one root cause for the incident report.

Determine the justified causal assessment and immediate action from the supplied evidence.

Constraints:

- protect stored inventory while diagnosis proceeds;
- zone-level interventions are allowed;
- do not use the open web;
- do not assume facts outside the supplied evidence.

Return the required normalized final-answer object.
